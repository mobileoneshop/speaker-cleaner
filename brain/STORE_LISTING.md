## Full description
Is your phone speaker muffled after dust or water exposure? SonicClean plays
precisely engineered sound frequencies and vibration patterns that shake dust
and water out of your speaker grills — no tools needed.

• AUTO CLEAN — one-tap 34-second deep clean with live progress
• MANUAL MODE — tune the frequency (50–500 Hz) to your device's sweet spot
• VIBRATION MODE — haptic patterns that dislodge stubborn dust
• SOUND TEST — check clarity right after cleaning
• Works 100% offline — your data never leaves your device

How to use: disconnect headphones, set volume to max, place the phone
speaker-down, and tap Start.

Note: results vary by device and blockage severity. Keep the phone away from
your ears during cleaning.
